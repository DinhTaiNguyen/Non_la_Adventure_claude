# 🏮 Nón Lá Love Adventure: Lanterns of Hội An

A cozy romantic co-op adventure through a magical Hội An lantern festival.
Two young hearts — **Joku** (wind 🌀) and **Jolie** (light ✨) — must work
together to bring the lost lantern flames back home.
*(Those are just the default names — you can rename the couple to yourselves
in the character-pick screen, the online screen, or the Wardrobe. ✏️)*

**100% self-contained web game** — plain HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript, no build
step, no assets to download. Works on computers **and** phones. Includes
**online co-op** so two people can play together from anywhere. 💞

---

## 🚀 Deploy on GitHub Pages (free)

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (e.g. `nonla-love-adventure`).
2. Upload **this entire folder** (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `README.md`).
3. In the repo: **Settings → Pages → Source: Deploy from a branch**,
   pick `main` branch, `/ (root)` folder, save.
4. After ~1 minute your game is live at
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/nonla-love-adventure/`
5. Send the link to your love. 💌

> Online co-op needs HTTPS — GitHub Pages provides it automatically.

### Run locally (optional)
Just open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static
server (`npx serve`, `python -m http.server`, VS Code Live Server…).

## 💞 Playing together online

1. Both open the game link (computer or phone — phones work great in landscape).
2. One of you: **Play Online Together → pick your character → Create a room**.
3. A 4-letter code appears — send it to your partner.
4. Partner: **Play Online Together → enter the code → Join.**
5. The host picks a chapter, and you're in the same world together!

Uses free PeerJS (WebRTC) — your devices talk directly to each other.
If a connection ever drops, lantern spirits (a friendly AI) guide your
partner's character until they return.

You can also play **2 Players on one keyboard**, or **Solo** with an AI
partner (press `Tab` to swap characters).

## 🎮 Controls

| | Joku (boy) | Jolie (girl) |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `A` / `D` | `◀` / `▶` |
| Jump | `W` | `▲` |
| Power | `E` — wind gust | `O` — light pulse |
| 2nd power | hold `Q` — shield | `P` — grow lotus |

**Together:** `H` hold hands · `Tab` swap (solo) · `1/2/3` emotes · `Esc` pause.
On phones, drag anywhere on the left side of the screen to walk left or right;
the action buttons stay under your right thumb.

## 🏮 How to play

- **Light the key lanterns** (🏮 counter) of each chapter, then reach the
  festival arch **together**.
- Joku's wind moves hanging lanterns, boats and crates; he shields the couple
  and channels **wind bridges** on the swirl marks.
- Jolie's light wakes lanterns, heals broken things, grows **lotus platforms**
  on sparkling water, and her aura reveals the dark.
- Grow the **Love Meter** by helping each other — it unlocks 5 bond powers,
  from Hand-in-Hand Walk to the Festival Blessing.
- Stand together under the big purple lanterns to relive **memories**. 💗
- Collect charms to unlock **8 áo dài costumes** in the Wardrobe
  (finish the story for a special one 👘).

## 🗺 Chapters

1. **Hội An Lantern Street** — learn the magic of wind & light
2. **Moon River Boat Ride** — sail the Thu Bồn under the moon
3. **Bamboo Bridge Village** — repair bridges, help the villagers
4. **Lotus Lake Promise** — lotus paths, shadow birds & strong winds
5. **Ancient Temple of Lanterns** — trust puzzles in golden halls
6. **The Great Lantern Festival** — combine your powers, light the night 🎆
