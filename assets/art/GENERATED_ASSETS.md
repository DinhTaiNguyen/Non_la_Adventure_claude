# Generated Vietnamese Art Library

These assets were created with Codex's built-in image generation mode, then cropped and optimized locally by `process_generated_assets.py`. Runtime files are WebP so the game does not download the large source masters during normal play.

## Prompt set

### Title couple

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: premium 16:9 title-screen hero artwork for a Vietnamese cooperative fairytale game. Primary request: a joyful young Vietnamese couple, one boy and one girl, standing close together beneath hundreds of glowing Hội An silk lanterns, each wearing an elegant colorful áo dài and a woven nón lá; they hold one shared lantern between them and look ready for an adventure together. Scene/backdrop: romantic Hội An old-town riverside at blue hour, ochre heritage houses, Thu Bồn river reflections, small wooden boat, distant lantern bridge, subtle bamboo and lotus details. Style/medium: exceptionally polished hand-painted 2.5D animated-film game art, refined gouache and watercolor textures with cinematic digital lighting, friendly stylized proportions, premium fairytale key art. Composition/framing: wide landscape, couple in the lower middle with clear silhouettes, open darker space around the center-top and side margins for game title and menu UI, layered foreground/midground/background. Lighting/mood: warm amber lantern glow against deep indigo twilight, romantic, adventurous, welcoming, magical but culturally grounded. Color palette: indigo, lantern amber, Hội An ochre, áo-dài blue and rose, lotus pink, small jade accents. Materials/textures: woven palm-leaf hats, silk áo dài, handmade silk lantern ribs, wet stone and river reflections. Constraints: recognizably Vietnamese; culturally respectful; no Chinese or Japanese temple motifs; no modern vehicles; no wedding ceremony; no text; no logo; no watermark; no UI mockup; both characters fully visible above the knees; clean faces and hands.
